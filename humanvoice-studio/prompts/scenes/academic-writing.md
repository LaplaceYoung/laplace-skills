# Scene: Academic Writing

## Use for
- Abstract and intro polishing
- Related-work synthesis
- Reviewer response draft
- Academic Chinese paraphrase

## Output shape
- Preserve argument structure
- Tighten logic links
- Keep technical terms accurate

## Prompt block
Rewrite for academic readability with reduced mechanical tone.
Do not weaken claims or alter evidence.
Keep terminology consistent.

Constraints:
- No absolute claims unless source supports them.
- No vague grand statements.
- Prefer precise verbs (e.g., "estimate", "improve", "compare") over hype words.

Optional mode:
- `journal`: formal and compact
- `conference`: concise and direct
- `review-response`: polite, specific, evidence-linked
