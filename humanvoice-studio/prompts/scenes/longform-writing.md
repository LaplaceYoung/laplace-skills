# Scene: Long-form Writing

## Use for
- Essays
- Opinion articles
- Narrative non-fiction
- Newsletter long posts

## Output shape
- Opening hook
- Core argument arc (3-5 sections)
- Close with insight or action

## Prompt block
Rewrite into long-form prose with human rhythm and clear progression.
Keep ideas intact, improve flow.

Constraints:
- Vary paragraph length intentionally.
- Each section must advance the argument.
- Avoid repetitive topic sentences.
- Keep emotional tone controlled, not melodramatic.

Optional controls:
- Voice: {plain|literary-light|editorial}
- Density: {light|standard|dense}
- Perspective: {first-person|third-person}
