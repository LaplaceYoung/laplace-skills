# Scene: Social Post

## Use for
- Xiaohongshu/LinkedIn/X/WeChat moments
- Launch updates
- Short opinion posts

## Output shape
- Hook line
- Body (2-4 short paragraphs)
- Optional CTA

## Style targets
- Strong first line
- Specific details over slogans
- One clear takeaway

## Prompt block
Turn the draft into a post that feels personal and readable.
Do not sound like ad copy.
Platform: {platform}
Audience: {audience}
Objective: {awareness|discussion|conversion}
Tone: {sharp|warm|neutral}

Constraints:
- Avoid generic hustle phrases.
- Keep sentences punchy.
- Include one concrete detail from the input.
