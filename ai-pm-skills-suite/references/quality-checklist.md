# Quality Checklist

## Structural
- Trigger signals are concrete and non-generic
- Activation conditions are explicit
- Do-not-use conditions are explicit
- Required / optional inputs are explicit
- Tool-category table is present and specific
- Workflow steps are concrete and actionable
- Output artifact shape is explicit
- Failure modes are documented
- Example prompt is included

## Content quality
- The skill produces a real PM artifact, not generic advice
- The tool guidance matches the artifact type
- The self-check covers assumptions and missing evidence
- The escalation path is clear when confidence is low

## Quantitative promotion targets
- Format compliance >= 95%
- Average rubric >= 85/100
- Manual rewrite rate <= 20%
- Critical hallucinations = 0
- CN/EN key-field consistency >= 95%
